Use case: stylized-concept.
Asset type: environment concept art for Plaguefall, a Brazilian cangaco cyberpunk arena shooter for Sega Mega Drive. Create ONE polished landscape production concept sheet, about 1536x1024, with a large arena environment occupying the top 80 percent and three isolated environmental prop/material studies along a clean dark bottom strip. One location only, not a collage of several levels. The environment is the subject; no people, no player, no enemies, no portraits, no UI, no health bars, no dialogue, no logo, no explanatory writing or labels.
Art direction: hand-painted production environment with disciplined angular silhouettes, rich but controlled brush texture, crisp material separation and readable masses that can later become 16-bit tiles. Brazilian semiarid industrial settlement, whitewashed masonry, exposed red brick and terracotta, rusted blue-grey machinery, salvaged functional technology, dry caatinga, mandacaru only at exterior boundaries. Warm ochre and aged bone contrasted with petrol-blue shadows; restrained green biological intrusion grows gradually through the campaign. Technology feels repaired and locally used, not generic neon city. Environmental narrative through physical evidence, wear, tracks and arrangement, readable without text.
Camera and playability: orthographic elevated view approximately 60 degrees above the ground, looking toward the back of a rectangular single-screen arena, no horizon or vanishing-point perspective, no dramatic low-angle shot. Show the complete floor footprint. Central 60 percent of the arena remains broad and navigable with quiet floor values; architectural story props concentrated at the perimeter, short front walls do not hide the floor, upper walls cut away where useful. Clear separation of decorative periphery, traversable ground and any hazard patches. This is a future screen design reference, not a promise of implemented scenery collision.
Continuity: introduce chapel architecture, a Madonna statue or a spacecraft ONLY when specifically requested in the phase scene below. There are no other statues or churches hidden in factory scenes. Alien nests are insect chitin, fibrous resin, eggs and consumed machinery; no tentacles or human gore.
Keep the three bottom studies large and useful to a future environment artist, same materials and lighting as the main scene. Opaque background. No legible text anywhere.
Specific phase: Fase 7 — Baixio das antenas.
Scene and prop studies: Semi-arid communications maintenance courtyard under squat patched dish antennas and low radio towers cropped at upper boundary, mandacaru and dry scrub outside the fence. Two brown mud basins at upper right and lower left. Tracks tell the discovery: parallel insect claw scoring, resin smears and dragged wire converge toward a single buried service-cable conduit pointing into the launch complex. Human repair history survives as spliced wires, taped ceramic insulators, reused water tank and a sun-bleached worker shelter at the perimeter. Empty broad courtyard, no ally silhouette or belongings suggesting Dinorah returned. Prop studies: cable splice with invasive chitin, muddy claw tracks converging, patched dish base and electrical insulator.
