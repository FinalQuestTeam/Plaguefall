Asset type: environment concept art for Plaguefall, a Brazilian cangaco cyberpunk arena shooter for Sega Mega Drive. One location only, not a collage of several levels. The environment is the subject; no people, no player, no enemies, no portraits, no UI, no health bars, no dialogue, no logo, no explanatory writing or labels.
Art direction: hand-painted production environment with disciplined angular silhouettes, rich but controlled brush texture, crisp material separation and readable masses that can later become 16-bit tiles. Brazilian semiarid industrial settlement, whitewashed masonry, exposed red brick and terracotta, rusted blue-grey machinery, salvaged functional technology, dry caatinga, mandacaru only at exterior boundaries. Warm ochre and aged bone contrasted with petrol-blue shadows; restrained green biological intrusion grows gradually through the campaign. Technology feels repaired and locally used, not generic neon city. Environmental narrative through physical evidence, wear, tracks and arrangement, readable without text.

Show the complete floor footprint. Central 60 percent of the arena remains broad and navigable with quiet floor values; architectural story props concentrated at the perimeter, short front walls do not hide the floor, upper walls cut away where useful. Clear separation of decorative periphery, traversable ground and any hazard patches. This is a future screen design reference, not a promise of implemented scenery collision.
