Use case: stylized-concept.
Asset type: approval preview of a single collectible sprite for Plaguefall, a Sega Mega Drive top-down arena shooter set in an industrial Brazilian sertão.
Primary request: draw one small recovery equipment satchel dropped after player death. A compact sturdy cangaço-inspired leather shoulder pouch resting upright on the ground, short arched carrying strap at the top, wide folded flap, one large pale rectangular central buckle. Immediately recognizable as a bag, with a clear compact silhouette. Elevated three-quarter top-down view, simplified to match flat pictographic item pickups.
Style: authentic extremely low resolution 16-bit game pixel art designed on a strict logical 16 by 16 pixel canvas, shown enlarged with crisp square pixels using nearest neighbor. All edges align to this coarse grid. Each logical pixel is a large uniform square. Use approximately 14 by 14 logical pixels for the bag leaving a transparent border. No subpixel details.
Color palette: ONLY three opaque flat colors: pale cream #DBDBB6 for continuous one-logical-pixel outer silhouette outline and buckle, golden ochre #DBB624 for the bag body, dark brown #492400 for the strap interior, flap seam and minimal deep shadows. All other pixels fully transparent. The cream outline completely surrounds the outer silhouette to match existing game collectibles. The strap has a visible transparent opening. Broad graphic shapes, readable at native 16x16 size. Avoid ornamental details.
Composition: exactly one bag centered, occupying most of the image, generous enough transparent margin to avoid clipping. Transparent background. No environment, no floor, no cast shadow, no glow, no lighting effects, no gradients, no antialiasing, no smooth curves, no text, no letters, no digits, no labels, no grid lines, no sprite sheet, no extra objects or weapons. This is an art proposal only, no UI mockup.
